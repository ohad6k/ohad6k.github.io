Barcelona 4-0 Osasuna: one possession, corner to goal

A Barcelona corner is headed clear, recovered on the right, worked to Messi, and ends in a left-foot shot from 20.1 yards that goes in. The whole possession takes 13.8 seconds. This replay rebuilds it from StatsBomb open data, and it says on screen which parts were recorded and which are filled in.

What this is: a visualization of event data plus 360 freeze-frames. It isn't tracking data, and nothing in it is read from video. No position was recorded during Messi's 3.46 second carry.

Measured

  • 10 moments, one per event timestamp that has a 360 freeze-frame. At each, every player inside the camera's view is placed where StatsBomb recorded them.
  • Event locations, pass end points, the shot location, xG and timestamps.

Filled in

  • All movement between the 10 moments, in straight eased lines, and the ball's arcs.
  • Players outside the camera's view, shown faded at a held position.
  • Run cycles, the keeper's dive, the ball in the net, the crowd and the slow motion. The 16 extra lines around the real shot illustrate xG 0.06; they are not data.
